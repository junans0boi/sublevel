Freesound recordings, cut into single footsteps (S-0008 ~ S-0022; Resources/Sfx: S-0038 ~ S-0045 and S-0047 ~ S-0051 in docs/ASSET_LEDGER.md).

Every source sound was checked on its Freesound page on 2026-11-16 and carried the Creative Commons 0 licence
(http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): copy, modify, distribute and perform, even commercially, without
asking the author. Source URL pattern: https://freesound.org/s/<id>/ . The ids and the original file names are in the ledger.

The clips here are mono 16-bit WAV cuts of those recordings (SoundCutTool); nothing else was changed. Original names with
trademarks are kept out of this folder (ADR-183).

Later files in this folder from other Freesound sounds are listed in the ledger with their own licence line; a
Creative Commons Attribution one is also shown on the credits screen (Credits.cs).
