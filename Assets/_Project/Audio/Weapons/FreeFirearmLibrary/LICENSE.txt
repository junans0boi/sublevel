Files here are single shots cut from the "Prepared SFX Library" near-distance recordings,
converted to 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono, normalised and renamed.

Resources/Shots/shot_*_far.wav (#120, ADR-092): the far "thump/echo" gunshot layer, cut from the library's
"mid distance" takes (pistol X_31P, smg G_20P, rifle D_24P, shotgun H_16P, sniper W_24P), mixed to mono 16-bit
44.1 kHz, low-passed, two late low slapbacks added, faded and normalised by tools/derive_g4_audio.py.
