Freesound recordings, cut into single gunshots, reloads and bullet whizzes (S-0023 ~ S-0037 in docs/ASSET_LEDGER.md).

Every sound is listed in the ledger with its Freesound id, uploader and licence. All are Creative Commons 0
(http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) except one: the pistol reload (S-0035) is Creative Commons
Attribution 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), shown on the credits screen (Credits.cs, ADR-177).

The clips here are mono 16-bit 44.1 kHz WAV cuts (tools/sound_cut.py, ranges in tools/sound_shots.json): cut, faded,
peak-normalised; the "far" clips are also low-passed and delayed. Original names with trademarks stay in the ledger only.
